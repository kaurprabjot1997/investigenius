How it works

Four agents. One verdict.

Prosecutor

Builds the case

Argues for suspicion, citing only this case's real transactions and accounts.

Defense

Finds the innocent read

Actively searches for the legitimate explanation before anyone commits to a verdict.

Adjudicator

Rules, with citations

Weighs both arguments. Every claim must cite real case data, or it doesn't count.

Precedent chat

Governance

Nothing here auto-executes.

CITE

Citation-validated

Every claim is checked against real case data after generation — an uncited claim is caught by code, not trusted on faith.

GATE

Confidence-gated

Low-confidence verdicts are forced to "needs review" regardless of what the model concluded.

HUMAN

Human-approved

The system drafts and recommends. A person always makes the final call — escalate, close, or reject.

AUDIT

Hash-chained

Every action writes to an audit log where tampering is cryptographically detectable, not just logged.
